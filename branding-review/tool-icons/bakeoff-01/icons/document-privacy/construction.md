# Document Privacy construction

- **Classification:** ToolNest custom / flagship.
- **Primary silhouette:** one document with a folded corner.
- **Semantic modifier:** an inspection lens crosses the document edge and examines sparse structural traces.
- **Why it is not a safety claim:** there is no shield, lock, check, or success mark.
- **Family grammar:** 24x24 viewBox, 2px centered stroke, round caps and joins, `fill="none"`, `stroke="currentColor"`.
- **Small-size decision:** only two short internal traces remain behind the lens. More detail made the 20px preview noisy.
- **Distinctness rule:** the circular lens belongs only to inspection/privacy tools; searchable text uses a scan-frame/text-layer construction instead.
