# Searchable PDF construction

- **Classification:** ToolNest custom / flagship.
- **Primary silhouette:** one document with a folded corner.
- **Semantic modifier:** four scan-frame corners surround two indexed text lines.
- **Why it differs from Document Privacy:** it has no magnifier; its central subject is a text layer being recognized/indexed rather than traces being inspected.
- **Family grammar:** 24x24 viewBox, 2px centered stroke, round caps and joins, `fill="none"`, `stroke="currentColor"`.
- **Small-size decision:** two text rows survive at 20px while keeping approximately 2px separation from the scan frame.
- **Identity cue:** scan-frame corners can become the controlled custom grammar for OCR/search-layer tools without becoming a category colour or badge.
